# Programme form information

## Participants section

The Participants section in **Programmes -> Create New Programme** is intentionally hidden for now.

The participant limit and participant waiver controls have not been deleted. They remain in `index.html` inside `#createProgrammeParticipantsSection` and can be restored by removing the `hidden` attribute from that container when the fields are needed again.

## Target Group

The Target Group dropdown in **Programmes -> Create New Programme** is limited to:

- General Public (including student)
- Students

Other target-group choices were removed from the dropdown.
